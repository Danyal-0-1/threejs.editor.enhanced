### Operator-exclusion demonstration (the number that goes in the paper)

`wildcard ::= '*'` and `sign ::= '+' | '-'` contain `*`/`+` **inside quotes**.
A naive `text.count('*')` over the W3C file counts 82; excluding quoted
literals gives the true Kleene-star count **6**. The table above uses the
excluded figure.
